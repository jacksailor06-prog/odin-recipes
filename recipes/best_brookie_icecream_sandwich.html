The Best Brookie Icecream Sandwich

Description

The Brookie is a delicious hybrid dessert that combines gooey brownies and cookies into a bar shaped treat. But have you ever tried taking your favorite ice-cream and smashing it between two brookies? This easy, ooey goey, mouth watering recipe will be an instant hit for those with a big sweet tooth. For the best tasting sandwich, it is reccomended to use vanilla ice-cream.

Ingredients

Cookie Layer

  • 1/2 cup butter, softened
  • 1/2 cup light brown sugar
  • 1/4 white sugar
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • tsp baking soda
  • 1 cup semisweet chocolate chips

Brownie Layer

  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1/2 cup butter, melted
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/3 cup cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 tsp baking powder
  • 1/8 tsp salt

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (or 175 degrees C) and grease a 9x13-inch baking dish
  2. Prepare Cookie Layer: Beat 1/2 cup butter, 1/2 cup brown sugar, 1/4 cup white sugar, and 1/2 tsp vanilla in a large bowl until creamy. Add one egg; beat untiul light and creamy, about 2 minutes.
  3. Whisk 1 1/4 cups flour, 1/2 tsp salt, and baking soda in a bowl. Gradually stir flour mixture into butter mixture until the dough is combined. Stir chocolate chips into dough. Spread dough evenly into the prepared baking dish; set aside.
  4. Prepare Brownie Layer: Stir 1 cup sugar, 1/2 cup melted butter, and 1 tsp vanilla in a bowl; add two eggs and beat well. Mix in cocoa powder until well-combined. Stir in 1/2 cup flour, baking powder, and 1/8 tsp salt until batter is combined. Pour over cookie layer and spread to cover completely.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean, 20-25 minutes. Cool completly before cutting into 20 bars. Enjoy!
